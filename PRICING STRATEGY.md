# PRICING STRATEGY

## Goal

The first launch goal is user growth while roughly breaking even. Do not optimize for maximum margin yet. Price low, keep usage capped, and collect real usage/cost data.

## Recommended Early Pricing

### Free Trial

- 7 days free
- No credit card required
- Suggested limits:
  - 3 summarizer runs/day
  - 20 AI chats/day
  - 5 summary refines/day

Purpose: remove friction and get people to try Discere.

### Early Access Plan

- $9/month
- Good for the first users while trying to spread adoption
- Suggested limits:
  - 5 summarizer runs/day
  - 40 AI chats/day
  - 10 summary refines/day

This likely breaks even for normal users if OpenAI cost is around $3-$7 per user per month.

### Pro Plan Later

- $19/month
- Higher limits
- Scheduled reports
- Attachment reading
- More contacts
- Better for sustainable business once users prove they will pay

## Important Rule

Avoid unlimited usage at $9/month. One heavy user can erase profit.

## Suggested Launch Structure

- First 50-100 users: $9/month Early Access
- Make clear: "Early access pricing for initial users"
- Keep usage caps quietly generous but safe
- Track actual OpenAI cost per user
- After real usage data, either:
  - keep $9 if average cost is low, or
  - move new users to $15-$19

## Break-Even Math

If average monthly cost is:

- $3/user/month: $9 is safe
- $5/user/month: $9 is okay, but support time is mostly unpaid
- $8/user/month: $9 barely works
- $10+/user/month: raise price or reduce usage limits

## Current Best Recommendation

Start at $9/month Early Access with a 7-day free trial.

Keep backend usage caps in place so the product can spread without allowing runaway OpenAI/API cost.
