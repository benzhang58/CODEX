# MARKETING BASICS

## Positioning

Discere is not a full inbox replacement. It is focused email intelligence for the people who matter most.

Core sentence:

> Discere summarizes emails from the important people you choose, so you can see what matters without digging through your inbox.

## Target Customers

Start with users who repeatedly monitor high-importance contacts:

- Founders tracking investors, customers, advisors, vendors, and partners
- Small business owners tracking suppliers, clients, and operations contacts
- Sales and business development users tracking key accounts
- Executives/operators tracking important internal and external conversations
- Researchers or project leads tracking collaborators and deadlines

Avoid marketing first to heavily regulated users such as healthcare, legal, finance, government, or enterprise security teams until compliance and legal review are stronger.

Headline:

> Built to be clear about data.

Copy:

> Discere reads mailbox data needed to summarize emails from contacts you choose. It stores account settings, summaries, processed message IDs, and limited source data needed to run the product. Email text needed for summaries is sent to AI. Attachment contents are only sent to AI if you enable attachment access.

> Read the Security FAQ

## One-Minute Demo Video Script

Goal: show value fast, then show trust controls.

0-5 seconds:

> "This is Discere. It summarizes emails from the important people you choose."

Show homepage and dashboard.

5-15 seconds:

> "Instead of scanning your entire inbox, add the contacts you care about."

Show Contacts popup and add one contact.

15-30 seconds:

> "Click play, and Discere checks recent emails from those contacts."

Show days back, play button, status loading, then summaries appearing.

30-42 seconds:

> "Each summary gives you the bottom line, main topics, action items, deadlines, and relevant attachments."

Open a summary card and scroll briefly.

42-52 seconds:

> "You can ask questions about your saved summaries or send yourself a report."

Show AI Assistant popup and Send Report button.

52-60 seconds:

> "You stay in control. Settings include data export, account deletion, AI attachment access, and privacy links."

Show Settings briefly, then return to dashboard.

Closing line:

> "Discere: email summaries for the people who matter."

## Launch Checklist For Marketing

- Record the one-minute demo.
- Add 5-10 realistic screenshots.
- Prepare a short privacy/security explanation.
- Prepare a simple FAQ answer for "what data do you read?"
- Reach out to 20 target users manually before broad public posting.
- Collect user feedback before changing pricing or widening the audience.
