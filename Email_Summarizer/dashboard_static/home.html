Data Use

How Discere uses your email.

What Discere reads Discere looks for emails from the people you choose to track. It does not summarize every email in your inbox.
What Discere saves Discere saves your contacts, settings, schedules, and summaries so you can come back and read them later.
What AI sees The email text needed for a summary is sent to AI so the summary can be written. Attachments are included only if you turn that setting on.
Report emails Summary reports are sent from Discere, not from your personal email account.
Deleting data You can delete summaries or your whole account. Older email text and attachments are removed from read or done summaries after the safety cleanup period.
