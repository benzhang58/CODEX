Data Use

What Discere reads, stores, and sends to AI.

What is read Discere reads mailbox data needed to find emails from the contacts you choose. For Gmail and Microsoft users, this happens through OAuth permissions. For other supported email providers, it uses the mailbox connection you enter.
What is stored Discere stores your account settings, tracked contacts, summaries, processed message identifiers, schedules, and limited source email data needed to show summaries and avoid duplicate processing.
What goes to AI Email text needed for summaries is sent to the AI provider. Attachment content is sent only if you turn on AI attachment access; otherwise Discere limits attachment use to metadata such as file names.
How deletion works You can delete summaries or your account. Read or done summaries have source bodies and attachments purged after the retention period, while processed IDs can remain so old emails are not summarized again by accident.
