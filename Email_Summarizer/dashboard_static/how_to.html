How To

Connect. Add contacts. Summarize. Ask AI.

The guide below explains how to connect your inbox, choose important contacts, generate summaries, ask AI questions about your emails, and manage privacy controls.

Open Discere
Quick Flow

Connect. Add contacts. Summarize. Ask AI.

  • Log in with Gmail or Microsoft, or create an account for another mailbox.
  • Add the senders you want Discere to track.
  • Run the summarizer or schedule recurring reports.
  • Use AI Assistant to ask questions about your emails.

Step-by-step guide

1

Log in or create an account

If you use Gmail or Outlook/Microsoft, use the matching sign-in button on the login page. Discere connects through OAuth, so you do not need to type a mailbox password into Discere for those providers.

If your mailbox is not Gmail or Microsoft, create an account with your email address first. Then open Settings and use Mailbox Connection to add the mailbox login details Discere should use.

2

Confirm your mailbox connection

Gmail users are connected through Gmail API read-only access. Microsoft users are connected through Microsoft OAuth and Microsoft-supported IMAP access. Non-OAuth mailboxes use the IMAP connection details saved in Settings.

Gmail and Microsoft users should not need the Mailbox Connection module. That module is only for standard mailbox accounts such as 263.com or other supported non-OAuth providers.

3

Add important contacts

Open the Contacts card in the dashboard and add the email addresses you care about. Discere is designed around tracked senders, not your entire inbox.

If you run the summarizer without contacts, Discere will ask you to add contacts first. This prevents accidental broad inbox scans.

4

Run the summarizer

Choose the Days Back value, then click the play button. Discere checks recent emails from your tracked contacts and creates structured summaries with key topics, developments, action items, deadlines, attachments, and a bottom line.

The summarizer may include relevant thread context so you can understand the conversation without opening every email manually.

5

Read, mark done, or delete summaries

A summary is considered new until you open and review it. Read or done summaries have source email bodies and saved attachments purged after 20 days, while summarized email IDs remain to prevent accidental duplicate summaries.

Use Mark as Done when you want Discere to remember the email was handled. If you delete a summary, Discere removes the related identifier, so that email can be rediscovered if you run the summarizer again.

6

Ask AI about your emails

Click AI Assistant in the dashboard to ask questions about saved summaries and source email context. You can ask things like "What deadlines do I have?", "What did Grant ask me to do?", or "Which attachments mention pricing?"

AI Assistant can also answer questions about Discere itself, including how summaries work, why deleted summaries can reappear, what data is sent to AI, how scheduled reports work, and where privacy controls live.

7

Send or combine reports

Select multiple summaries when you want to combine them into one report. Email reports are sent from Discere's configured report sender, intended to be Discere <discereresearch@gmail.com>, not from your connected mailbox.

This keeps Gmail, Microsoft, and non-OAuth users on the same report-sending path.

8

Schedule recurring reports

Click the clock button to create a scheduled report. Choose the report name, repeat cadence, send time, timezone, and Days Back value.

Each scheduled run creates a fresh summarizer check first, then emails the full formatted report to your connected account email. Saved schedules can be viewed, edited, toggled on/off, or deleted.

9

Control attachments and summary style

In Settings, AI Attachment Access controls whether attachment contents can be sent to AI. When it is off, Discere limits attachment use to metadata such as filenames.

Summary Preferences let you save reusable instructions for future summaries, such as focusing more on deadlines, commercial terms, or action items.

10

Delete your account or report issues

Settings includes account deletion, privacy links, and bug reporting.

You can also revoke Google access from Google Account permissions. Microsoft access can be revoked from Microsoft account consent management or Microsoft My Apps, depending on the account type.

Common questions

Why did an email come back after I deleted the summary?

Deleting a summary removes the saved summary and related identifier so the email can be rediscovered later. If you want Discere to remember that the email was handled, mark the summary as Done instead of deleting it.

Can AI Assistant answer privacy or security questions?

Yes. AI Assistant can answer questions about saved emails and about Discere’s workflow, privacy policy, security FAQ, terms, report sending, retention behavior, and account controls.

Does Discere summarize every email?

No. Discere is designed to summarize emails from the contacts you choose. It verifies the parsed sender before processing so unrelated senders should not be summarized as tracked contacts.

What gets sent to AI?

Relevant email text and thread context are sent to OpenAI for summarization. Attachment contents are included only if AI Attachment Access is enabled.
