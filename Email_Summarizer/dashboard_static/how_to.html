How To

Connect. Add contacts. Summarize. Ask AI.

The guide below shows how to connect your inbox, choose contacts, summarize emails, ask AI questions, and manage privacy controls.

Open Discere
Quick Flow

Connect. Add contacts. Summarize. Ask AI.

  • Log in with Gmail or Microsoft.
  • Add the senders you want Discere to track.
  • Run the summarizer or schedule recurring reports.
  • Use AI Assistant to ask questions about your emails.

Step-by-step guide

1

Log in with Gmail or Microsoft

Use the Gmail or Microsoft sign-in button. Discere connects securely through those providers, so you do not type your mailbox password into Discere.

2

Confirm your mailbox connection

Gmail connects through Google. Microsoft and Outlook connect through Microsoft. Once connected, Discere can check the emails needed for your summaries.

3

Add important contacts

Open the Contacts card and add the senders you care about. Discere focuses on those people, not your whole inbox.

If there are no contacts, Discere will ask you to add one before running.

4

Run the summarizer

Choose Days Back, then click the play button. Discere checks recent emails from your contacts and creates summaries with key topics, updates, action items, deadlines, attachments, and a bottom line.

It may include thread context so you can understand the conversation quickly.

5

Read, mark done, or delete summaries

Summaries stay new until you open them. Read or done summaries keep the summary, but source email bodies and saved attachments are purged after 20 days.

Use Mark as Done when an email is handled. If you delete a summary, that email can be found again the next time you run the summarizer.

6

Ask AI about your emails

Click AI Assistant to ask about saved summaries and email context. Try questions like "What deadlines do I have?" or "What did Grant ask me to do?"

It can also explain Discere features, privacy controls, scheduled reports, and why deleted summaries can reappear.

7

Send or combine reports

Select summaries to send their Executive Summary sections in one email report. Reports are sent from Discere, not from your personal mailbox.

8

Schedule recurring reports

Click the clock button to schedule reports. Choose the name, repeat timing, send time, timezone, and Days Back.

Each run checks for new summaries first, then sends the report to your connected email. You can edit, pause, or delete saved schedules.

9

Control attachments and summary style

In Settings, AI Attachment Access controls whether attachment contents can be sent to AI. When off, only attachment names are used.

Summary Preferences let you save instructions for future summaries, like focusing more on deadlines or action items.

10

Delete your account or report issues

Settings includes account deletion, privacy links, and bug reporting.

You can also revoke Google or Microsoft access from your provider account settings.

Common questions

Why did an email come back after I deleted the summary?

Deleting removes the saved summary and lets the email be found again later. To remember it was handled, mark it as Done instead.

Can AI Assistant answer privacy or security questions?

Yes. It can answer questions about saved emails, privacy, security, report sending, retention, and account controls.

Does Discere summarize every email?

No. Discere summarizes emails from contacts you choose and verifies the sender before processing. Those contacts are not notified.

What gets sent to AI?

Relevant email text and thread context are sent to OpenAI. Attachment contents are sent only if AI Attachment Access is on.
