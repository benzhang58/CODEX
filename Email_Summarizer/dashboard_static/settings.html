FAQ

Answers to the questions business users usually ask before trusting an email service.

Where does my email data go?

Your mailbox is accessed by Discere so we can find emails from the contacts you track and generate summaries from those threads.

To generate summaries, relevant email content is sent to OpenAI through the OpenAI API. If AI attachment access is turned on, attachment content can be included too.

OpenAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train or improve OpenAI models by default unless the API organization explicitly opts in. OpenAI may still retain limited API data, including prompts and responses, for abuse monitoring and safety under its published API data controls.

Who can see my data?

Your saved summaries, thread data, and attachments are scoped to your account inside Discere.

Other Discere users cannot see your account data. Discere processes and stores the data needed to run your account, and OpenAI receives the content needed to generate summaries through the OpenAI API.

Are attachments sent to AI?

Only if you turn on AI Attachment Access.

If it is off, Discere does not send attachment contents to AI. Discere only sends the email thread text and basic attachment names, such as filenames.

How long is source email data kept?

Read or done summaries have source email bodies and saved attachments purged after 20 days, while summarized email IDs remain to prevent accidental duplicate summaries.

Can I revoke Gmail or Microsoft access?

Yes. You can revoke Google access from Google Account permissions. Microsoft access can be revoked from Microsoft account consent management or Microsoft My Apps, depending on whether you use a personal or work/school Microsoft account.

Revoking access can disable mailbox summaries or scheduled reports until you reconnect.

What are the main privacy and security risks?

The main risks are Discere storing email-derived summary data, relevant email text being sent to OpenAI through the OpenAI API for summarization, and report emails being delivered from Discere to your connected account email.

If you do not want summary content included in report emails, use Email Notification under Scheduled Summaries.

This is reasonable for many normal workflows, but highly confidential legal, finance, board, or executive use should be evaluated more carefully.

Is this a formal privacy policy or enterprise security certification?

No. This FAQ is an overview, not a legal policy or enterprise certification.

Businesses that need a formal review should request a privacy policy, retention explanation, and a written security overview before production rollout.

Can I control what the summaries emphasize?

Yes. Use Summary Preferences to save reusable instructions such as focusing more on deadlines, asks, or action items.

You can also use Refine Summary on individual summaries without changing the underlying source thread.
