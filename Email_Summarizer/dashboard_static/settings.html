These are reusable instructions the app uses when generating future summaries.

Mailbox Connection

Use this only if your email is not Gmail or Outlook. This updates the mailbox credentials the summarizer uses. It does not change your dashboard login password.

How to

1
Sign in the right way

Use Google for Gmail and Microsoft for Outlook. If your email is not Gmail or Outlook, create a regular account first.

2
Set mailbox access if needed

If your email is not Gmail or Outlook, open Mailbox Connection in Settings and enter your real email login details there.

3
Add contacts

Add the people you want to track in Contacts. You can add them one by one or review the saved list in the popup.

4
Run the summarizer

Click Run Summarizer to check recent emails from those contacts. New summaries will appear on the left side of the dashboard.

5
Read and combine

Click a summary to open it. Check more than one summary if you want the right side to combine them automatically.

6
Ask follow-up questions

Use AI Assistant to ask simple questions about deadlines, asks, or attachment-related details from your saved summaries.

7
Refine the writing style

Use Refine Summary when you want the summaries to be shorter, clearer, more deadline-focused, or better organized for you.
