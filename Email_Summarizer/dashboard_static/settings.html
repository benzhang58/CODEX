How to

1
Sign in the right way

Log in via Gmail/Outlook, or create an account.

2
Set mailbox access if needed

If your email is not Gmail or Outlook, open Mailbox Connection in Settings and enter your real email login details there.

3
Add contacts

Add the people you want to track in Contacts. You can add them one by one or review the saved list in the popup.

4
Run the summarizer

Click Run Summarizer to check recent emails from those contacts. New summaries will appear on the left side of the dashboard.

5
Read and combine

Click a summary to open it. Check more than one summary if you want the right side to combine them automatically.

6
Ask follow-up questions

Use AI Assistant to ask simple questions about deadlines, asks, or attachment-related details from your saved summaries.

7
Refine the writing style

Use Refine Summary when you want the summaries to be shorter, clearer, more deadline-focused, or better organized for you.

FAQ

Plain-English answers to the questions business users usually ask before trusting an email product.

Where does my email data go?

Your mailbox is accessed by the app so it can find emails from the contacts you track and generate summaries from those threads.

To generate summaries, the relevant email content is sent to the AI provider through the API. If AI attachment access is turned on, attachment content can be included too.

Who can see my data?

Your saved summaries, thread data, and attachments are scoped to your account inside the product.

The app server processes the data, and the AI provider receives the content needed to generate summaries. Other users should not be able to see your account data through the product UI.

Are attachments sent to AI?

Only if you turn on AI Attachment Access.

If it is off, the app withholds attachment contents from the AI and only uses the email thread plus basic attachment references.

How long is source email data kept?

Once a summary has been read, the app keeps the processed email ID so it does not summarize the same email again by accident.

After 7 days, the stored source thread body and saved attachments for that read summary are purged automatically for safety, unless you delete the summary manually first.

What are the main privacy and security risks?

The main risks are server-side storage of email-derived data, third-party AI processing of summary inputs, and any future delivery paths such as emailed reports or texted PDF links.

This is reasonable for many normal workflows, but highly confidential legal, finance, board, or executive use should be evaluated more carefully.

Is this a formal privacy policy or enterprise security certification?

No. This FAQ is a plain-English overview, not a legal policy or enterprise certification.

Businesses that need a formal review should request a privacy policy, retention explanation, and a written security overview before production rollout.

Can I control what the summaries emphasize?

Yes. Use Summary Preferences to save reusable instructions such as focusing more on deadlines, asks, or action items.

You can also use Refine Summary on individual summaries without changing the underlying source thread.
