Privacy Policy

How Discere handles account and email data

This Privacy Policy explains how Discere accesses, uses, stores, shares, and deletes information when you use the product.

Effective date: April 23, 2026
Last updated: April 23, 2026

If you have questions about this policy, privacy requests, or security concerns, contact discereresearch@gmail.com.

1. Scope

This policy applies to Discere’s website, dashboard, connected email flows, summary generation, scheduled reports, and related support or analytics functions.

Discere is the product and service name. The service is operated by Discere Research.

It does not apply to third-party services that you use separately outside Discere, even if those services integrate with the product.

2. Information Discere collects

  • Account information such as email address, sign-in method, and profile name
  • Connected mailbox information needed to authenticate Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365, or other supported providers
  • Email thread data needed to detect tracked contacts and generate summaries
  • Attachment names and, if enabled, attachment content previews or extracted attachment text
  • Product settings such as tracked contacts, summary preferences, scheduling choices, timezone, and delivery settings
  • Operational data such as analytics events, bug reports, page metadata, and service logs

3. How Discere uses information

  • Authenticate users and maintain account access
  • Read supported mailbox data needed to identify relevant emails from tracked contacts
  • Generate, refine, and deliver structured summaries and combined reports
  • Support user settings such as tracked contacts, AI attachment access, and summary preferences
  • Send scheduled reports by email or phone where configured
  • Operate, secure, debug, monitor, and improve the product

4. Google and Microsoft user data

When you connect a Google or Microsoft account, Discere uses OAuth-based permissions to access the scopes you grant. Discere uses that access only to operate the features you request, such as reading mailbox content for summaries or sending a report from your connected account where supported.

Discere does not sell Google or Microsoft user data. Discere does not use Gmail or Microsoft mailbox data for advertising. Discere does not transfer Google Workspace API data to third parties except as needed to operate the requested product functions, comply with law, enforce terms, or protect the service.

5. AI processing

Discere uses the OpenAI API to generate summaries and related AI output. Email thread content is sent to the AI provider for that purpose.

If AI attachment access is turned on, attachment content may also be included in AI processing. If AI attachment access is turned off, Discere limits AI input to email-thread text and attachment metadata such as file names.

AI output can be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should review generated summaries before relying on them for legal, financial, operational, or other sensitive decisions.

6. Sharing and service providers

Discere may share information with service providers strictly as needed to operate the product.

  • OpenAI for AI summarization and related model output
  • Render or comparable infrastructure providers for application hosting and storage
  • Twilio or comparable messaging providers for SMS or MMS delivery where enabled
  • Google and Microsoft for OAuth and mailbox-related access where you choose to connect those providers

Discere may also disclose information where required by law, to respond to valid legal process, to enforce its terms, or to protect users, the product, or the public.

7. Storage and retention

Discere stores summary data, configuration data, and limited operational data needed for product behavior.

  • Unread summaries are retained and are not automatically purged under the read-or-done retention rule.
  • For summaries marked as read or done, stored source thread bodies and saved attachments are purged after 20 days.
  • Processed message identifiers are retained after purge so previously handled emails are not re-summarized accidentally.
  • If a user manually deletes a summary, the related processed identifiers are removed so the email can be rediscovered later.
  • Bug reports, analytics events, and service logs may be retained for operational, support, or security purposes.

8. Security

Discere uses account-scoped access controls so users can access only their own summaries, contacts, settings, attachments, and related account data. Sensitive credentials and OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted form.

No internet-based service is completely secure. Discere cannot guarantee absolute security. Users handling highly confidential, regulated, or mission-critical data should evaluate whether the current product architecture is appropriate for their use case before connecting that information.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You can update tracked contacts, summary preferences, schedule settings, and AI attachment access in the product.
  • You can delete summaries. Deleting a summary also removes the related processed identifiers so the email can be rediscovered later.
  • You can delete your account using the account controls currently provided in the product.
  • You can revoke Google or Microsoft OAuth access from your provider account settings.

If you need deletion help, export assistance, or a privacy request workflow for launch, add a formal support address and internal response process before treating this policy as final.

10. Children’s privacy

Discere is not intended for children under 13, and it is not designed for use by children. Do not use the product if you are not old enough to do so under applicable law.

11. International use

Discere may process and store information in jurisdictions other than your own. By using the product, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those jurisdictions, subject to applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

Discere may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes should be reflected on this page, including a revised effective or last-updated date.
