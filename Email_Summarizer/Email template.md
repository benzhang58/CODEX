I made a tool called Discere: [website link]

I originally built it for my parents because they’re really busy and get a lot of emails they don’t always have time to keep up with. The basic idea is that you choose the people whose emails you really don’t want to miss, and Discere helps summarize those emails so you can quickly see what matters, what needs a reply, and whether there are any deadlines or action items.

It’s free for invited early users while I keep improving it. I’m not trying to sell you on it or anything. I just thought it might be useful for people who have too much going on in their inbox and want a simpler way to stay on top of the important stuff.
